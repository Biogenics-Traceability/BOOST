# Publications

Guides and reports published by the BOOST Community Group. Each file carries its version and date in its colophon; the current version is the one linked from the site's landing page.

| Document | Version | Date | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Plain-Language Guide to the BOOST Specification](BOOST_Plain_Language_Guide_v1.1.1.pdf) — what the specification is, what it records, and how foresters, facilities and landowners would meet it | 1.1.1 | November 2026 | [W3C Software and Document License](https://www.w3.org/copyright/software-license/) |

The Guide was prepared under contract SPPD25024 with the Mariposa County Resource Conservation District, funded by the Governor's Office of Land Use and Climate Innovation. The research behind it, the Regulatory Program Alignment Report and the regulatory sufficiency assessments, is published by Arbos at <https://arbos.bio/research/>.
