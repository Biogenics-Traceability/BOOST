**Title:** Charter amendment vote: extend scope to timber and forest fiber (PR #300)

This issue records the Community Group's vote on the amended charter proposed in #300, under § Amendments to this Charter.

**The proposal.** The charter's scope extends from solid biomass for energy applications to forest-derived materials: woody biomass for energy, sawn timber and engineered wood products, and forest fiber for pulp, paper and bio-based manufacturing. Solid biomass for energy from other sources stays in scope. The amendment adds scope and removes none. The full text is the `BOOST_Charter.org` diff in #300, as of commit `214eff1`.

**The vote.**
- Opens: *[meeting date]*. Closes: *[meeting date + 30 days]*.
- Passes with two-thirds of votes cast. Abstentions are noted and not counted.
- The amended charter takes effect 7 days after the result is announced.

**How to vote.** Comment on this issue with **Approve**, **Reject** or **Abstain**, plus your justification. The charter requires one with every vote. One vote per participant. Votes may be changed by a later comment before the close.
